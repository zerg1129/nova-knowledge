先認識這間酒廠 Meet the Distillery

Wonseok Whisky Distillery 是韓國小型獨立單一麥芽威士忌蒸餾廠，由創辦人暨首席蒸餾師 Wonseok Kim 創立並親自營運。 Wonseok Whisky Distillery is a small independent Korean single malt whisky distillery founded and operated by Wonseok Kim, Founder and Master Distiller.

酒廠於 2025 年 2 月開始商業蒸餾。從碾麥、糖化、發酵、蒸餾、橡木桶管理到調和準備，每個關鍵流程都由創辦人親手執行。 Commercial distillation began in February 2025. From milling, mashing and fermentation to distillation, cask management and blending preparation, every key process is handled by the founder himself.

酒廠特色是一人制手工蒸餾、直接控制、近距離觀察與逐批次判斷；生產方式同時結合工程數據與感官經驗。 The distillery is built around one-person craft production, direct control, close observation and batch-by-batch decision making, guided by both engineering data and sensory experience.

發酵不是固定排程，而是依每一批次的狀態與性格調整。蒸餾以緩慢節奏進行，目標是增加與銅製壺式蒸餾器的接觸，降低不理想雜味，創造更乾淨、更緻密、口感更具質地的原酒。 Fermentation is not fixed by schedule; it is adjusted to each batch. Distillation is carried out slowly to increase copper contact, reduce unwanted off-notes and create a cleaner, denser, more textured spirit.

較完整的酒廠介紹 Fuller distillery introduction

Wonseok Whisky Distillery 是一間韓國小型獨立單一麥芽威士忌蒸餾廠，由 Wonseok Kim 創立並親自營運。作為一人制手工蒸餾廠，酒廠以直接控制、近距離觀察與逐批次判斷為核心，目標是打造酒體緻密、帶堅果感，且具鮮明韓國特色的單一麥芽原酒。

Wonseok Whisky Distillery is a small independent Korean single malt whisky distillery founded and operated by Wonseok Kim. Built as a one-person craft distillery, it is defined by direct control, close observation and batch-by-batch decision making, with the goal of creating a dense, nutty and distinctly Korean single malt spirit.

較完整的製程說明 Fuller production philosophy

發酵會透過比重、酸度、溫度與味覺進行監控。時間並非依固定排程決定，而是依每一批次的狀態與性格調整。熟成則受到韓國鮮明四季影響，從冬季約 -20°C 到夏季約 +40°C，橡木桶經歷顯著季節溫差，促進酒液與橡木之間的互動。

Fermentation is monitored through gravity, acidity, temperature and taste. The timing is adjusted according to the condition and character of each batch. Maturation is shaped by Korea's four seasons, from around -20°C in winter to +40°C in summer, creating active interaction between spirit and oak.

NEW BORN Double Oloroso 是什麼？ What is NEW BORN Double Oloroso?

NEW BORN Double Oloroso 是 Wonseok Whisky Distillery 的另一個酒款介紹。內容提到兩只 Oloroso hogshead 的酒液會先調合，使其風格達到平衡；混合後再分回兩只 Oloroso hogshead 中繼續熟成，裝瓶前再次調合成最終小批次作品。

NEW BORN Double Oloroso is a separate product introduction from Wonseok Whisky Distillery. The two Oloroso hogsheads are first married to bring their characters into balance, divided back into cask for further maturation, and married again before bottling.
